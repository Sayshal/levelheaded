## What You Get

**Level Doctor:** Checks a scene for the problems that make levels misbehave: missing floor surfaces, placeables with no level, tokens outside their level, stairs that lead nowhere, and level images at the wrong size. Most issues have a one-click fix, and Fix All handles the lot. Open it from the token controls or the Scene Directory.

<img src="https://wiki.3deathsaves.com/levelheaded/level-doctor.png" alt="Level Doctor" width="750">

**Floor Stack & Scene Merge:** Build several floors of equal height in one go from the Levels tab of Scene Config, with floor surfaces between them and each floor able to see its neighbours. Already drew each floor as its own scene? Merge them into one scene as levels from the Scene Directory, walls, lights and tokens included.

<img src="https://wiki.3deathsaves.com/levelheaded/floor-stack.png" alt="Floor Stack" width="750">

**Elevation & Level Sync:** Tokens move to the matching level when their elevation changes, and new tokens land on the level you're viewing. The Token HUD level picker can keep a token's height relative to the floor, put it on the floor, or keep its absolute elevation.

**Stairs & Templates:** Turn selected regions into stairs or an elevator with one tool. Stairs move tokens between levels without core's confirmation dialog, with an optional landing offset. Region templates set up roofs, balconies, trapdoors you can open and close, and vision-and-fade pairs.

<img src="https://wiki.3deathsaves.com/levelheaded/make-stair.png" alt="Make Stair" width="750">

## Also Included

- **Labels on Other Levels:** Dim or hide nameplates, bars and tooltips of tokens on other levels, and show elevation as relative or absolute.
- **Roof Fading:** Pick which tokens fade roofs, separately for GMs and players, with an optional hover delay.
- **GM View:** Hide every level above the viewed one, step between levels with keybinds, and select tokens on other levels without jumping the view.
- **Player Follow:** A player's view follows their token between levels, and GMs can pull a player to the level they're viewing.
- **Per-Level Environment:** Give a level its own darkness, global light and weather in Level Config.
- **Bulk Level Tools:** Assign, copy or move selected walls, lights, tiles and other placeables between levels.

## Installation

Find **Levelheaded** in Foundry's Module Browser, or paste this manifest URL:

```

```

Requires Foundry v14 and [3DS:ATLAS](https://github.com/Sayshal/3ds-atlas).
