## New Features

- Level Doctor audits a scene's levels, groups issues by severity, and fixes the ones it can, one at a time or all at once.
- Connect Levels tool turns selected regions into a stair between two levels or an elevator that stops at every level between.
- Merge Scenes as Levels stacks other scenes onto a scene as new levels, copying their placeables.
- Surface Template tool applies a roof, balcony, trapdoor or vision and fade pair to selected regions.
- Tokens that change elevation move to the level covering that elevation.
